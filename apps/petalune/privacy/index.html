Petalune privacy policy · Last updated 6 October 2026

Petalune: Lotus Gardens is developed by Yas Merak. It is an offline logic puzzle game for iPhone and iPad. The app does not require an account, include advertising, or send gameplay data to a developer-operated server.

Information stored on your device

Petalune saves your puzzle placements, notes, timers, undo history, completed puzzles, garden blooms, daily-puzzle access, and preferences locally so you can continue playing. The app does not transmit this information to us. Petalune does not provide app-managed cloud sync.

You can clear puzzle progress using Your comfort → Reset all progress. This keeps your comfort preferences. Deleting the app can also remove its local data. Device backups and any backup restoration are controlled by your Apple settings.

Optional purchases

Apple handles the optional Full Garden purchase through the App Store. Petalune uses Apple’s StoreKit to check your purchase entitlement and restore access. We do not receive your payment-card details or Apple Account password.

Apple processes purchase information under its own privacy policy. Purchases and restoration may require an internet connection.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Petalune does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Apple may provide App Store reports or diagnostics through its services, according to your Apple settings.

When you contact support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to share, such as a problem description or screenshot. We use that information to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. Email is processed by the email services used to deliver and store the correspondence.

We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent. Please avoid sending sensitive information, Apple Account passwords, or payment-card details.

This website

These portfolio pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in its GitHub Pages documentation and privacy statement.

The website loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which involves requests to Google’s servers. See Google Fonts privacy information. These pages do not include a contact form, advertising, analytics scripts, or cookies set by our page code. Email links open your chosen email app; visiting a page alone does not send us a support message.

Changes to this policy

If Petalune’s data practices change, this policy will be updated. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Yas Merak at yasind@ymail.com.

You can also visit the Petalune support page.
