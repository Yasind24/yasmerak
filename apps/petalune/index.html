Simple rules.
Room to think.

Every row, column, and outlined garden bed needs exactly one lotus. Flowers cannot touch, even at a corner. There’s no rush: mark empty cells, try a deduction, and come back to your saved puzzle whenever you like.

A QUIET GARDEN OF LOGIC

One lotus. Each way.

01

Find a place to bloom

Plant one lotus in each row, column, and garden bed. Give every flower space, including diagonally.

02

Follow the reasoning

Tap to plant, drag to mark empty cells, and undo a move. Teaching hints explain deductions when you want a little help.

03

Grow your garden

Each solved puzzle adds a bloom to your garden. No watering, waiting, or streak to keep. Just small moments, gathered.
