Common questions

How do I draw a province?

Drag between opposite corners. Each rectangle must hold exactly one number and contain that many cells. You can also choose Draw province, tap two corners, and confirm. If a draft is too small, lift your finger and continue it with another stroke.

What do the links and frontiers mean?

Golden links keep the two joined cells in the same province. Double boundaries marked with × keep the cells apart. Walls and towers mark territory you have claimed.

Can I get a hint?

Yes. Hints are free and unlimited. Reveal an observation, then show a placement, then choose whether to place it. If an existing province conflicts with the solution, you choose whether to remove it. Undo and Reset are always available.

What is free, and what does Full Campaign unlock?

Three training missions, the first 20 Borderlands realms, today’s daily conquest, unlimited hints, and your atlas are free. One optional non-consumable Full Campaign purchase opens the five other kingdoms and the daily archive. It is not a subscription, and there are no ads. Your regional price appears in the app before purchase.

How do I restore my purchase?

Connect to the App Store using the Apple Account that made the purchase, then open Settings → Restore Purchases. Restoring unlocks purchased content; it does not restore puzzle progress deleted from the device.

My purchase is pending or hasn’t unlocked the kingdoms. What next?

If Apple is awaiting approval, access unlocks when Apple confirms the purchase. Otherwise, check your connection and Apple Account, then try Restore Purchases. If access is still missing, email support with the message shown in the app.

When does the daily conquest change?

At midnight UTC; the app shows the matching local time. A board you already opened stays on its original date across the rollover. The bundled pool of 366 boards repeats until the app is updated.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Puzzles, artwork, and audio are bundled in the app. Buying or restoring Full Campaign requires an App Store connection; unlocked gameplay works offline.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress is saved locally on your iPhone after each move, including your atlas and preferences. There is no app account or app-operated cloud sync. Deleting the app can remove local progress, so avoid it as a troubleshooting step. If saving fails, the app shows Retry saving instead of claiming success.

How do I reset my progress?

Open Settings → Delete local progress and confirm. This removes saved boards and your atlas from this iPhone. It cannot be undone and does not cancel your Full Campaign purchase, which you can restore at any time.

How do I change motion, contrast, or sound?

Open Settings to turn Sound and Haptics on or off, enable Reduce motion (walls and towers then appear finished), switch on high contrast with province patterns, adjust map detail, and set optional daily or campaign reminders.
