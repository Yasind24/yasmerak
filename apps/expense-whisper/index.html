MY FIRST iOS APP

A simple personal expense tracker for the little transactions that add up. Tap the mic, say what you spent or earned, and keep your money log close at hand.

Download on the App Store ↗

For iPhone and iPad · Requires iOS 17.6 or later

Less setup.
More keeping up.

Expense Whisper is built for a personal money log. Add expenses and income, review your balance, and see how the week, month, or year is shaping up. Your transactions are stored on your device, with no account to create.

01 / SPEAK & SAVE

Voice makes it quick.

Use the microphone to log an expense or income. Review your transactions, edit the details, and use search and filters to find what you need.

02 / SEE THE PATTERN

A clearer picture.

Switch between weekly, monthly, and yearly summaries. Export transaction files or share a PNG summary report when you want to keep a copy.

03 / KEEP IT CLOSE

On your device.

Keep your transaction history locally, use home screen widgets, and choose dark mode. The app works offline and doesn’t require an account.
