PIP privacy policy · Last updated 7 October 2026

PIP: Robot Puzzle Adventure is developed by Yas Merak. PIP stores puzzle progress, preferences, achievements, and a limited playtest diagnostic log locally on your device. PIP does not send this information to the developer automatically and does not include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Information stored on your device

Your current puzzle, undo history, completed rooms, achievements, and preferences stay on your device so you can continue playing. PIP does not provide app-managed cloud sync.

Resetting the journey clears puzzle progress and achievements. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to device backups managed by Apple. Purchase ownership is managed by your Apple Account and can be restored through Apple.

Optional diagnostic sharing

You can choose to export playtest diagnostics through Settings. The file includes event names, times, app version, room numbers, and purchase status/environment. It does not include your Apple Account credentials, advertising identifier, or puzzle-related personal content.

Sharing sends the file to the recipient you select in the system share sheet. Only share with a recipient you trust. PIP does not upload this log automatically.

Purchases and restoration

Purchases and restoration are processed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles payment details and purchase records under its policies. PIP receives verified purchase information to determine access to Full Journey and can record the result locally. We do not receive your Apple Account password or payment-card details.

Apple processes purchases under its privacy policy. An App Store connection is needed to purchase or restore.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

PIP does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. Its limited diagnostic log stays on your device unless you choose to share it. Apple may provide App Store reports or diagnostics through its services, according to your Apple settings.

When you contact support

If you email Yas, we receive your email address and the information you choose to share, such as a screenshot or diagnostic file. We use this information to respond and troubleshoot. Email is processed by the services that deliver and store the correspondence.

We keep support correspondence only as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent. Please avoid sending sensitive information, Apple Account passwords, or payment-card details.

This website

These portfolio pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security, as described in its GitHub Pages documentation and privacy statement.

The website loads its typeface from Google Fonts, which involves requests to Google’s servers. See Google Fonts privacy information. These pages do not include a contact form, advertising, analytics scripts, or cookies set by our page code. Email links open your chosen email app; visiting a page alone does not send us a support message.

Changes to this policy

If PIP’s data practices change, this policy will be updated. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Yas Merak at yasind@ymail.com, or visit PIP support.
