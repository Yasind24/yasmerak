SMALL STEPS. CLEVER CONNECTIONS.

Push. Connect. Power restored.

01

Find your way around

Tap a floor tile and Pip walks there. Swipe for a single step, use the directional controls, or play with arrow keys and WASD on a hardware keyboard.

02

Give each cell room

Stand beside a power cell and tap to push. Pip can push one cell at a time, but can’t pull. Keep a route open to reach the side you need.

03

Wake up the machine

Fill every socket to restore the room. Take the next step toward daylight, or replay a room to find a more thoughtful route.

ROOM TO EXPERIMENT

A wrong turn is just a turn.

No timers or lives. Undo as often as you need, restart a room, or ask for a hint based on your current position. A trapped cell? Give it room with a few steps back.

YOUR CLEVEREST ROUTE

A little extra challenge.

Every room has a minimum-move target. Earn hint-free and minimum-move achievements, revisit completed rooms, and see your progress in Your stats.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE

Pick up where you left off.

Progress and undo history save on your device. Puzzles, artwork, and music work offline, with separate sound, music, haptics, and reduced-motion controls.
